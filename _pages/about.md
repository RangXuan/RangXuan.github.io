I am a first-year Ph.D. student at the School of Mechanical and Electrical Engineering of Kunming University of Science and Technology. I earned my Bachelor of Engineering degree from the College of Information Engineering and Automation at Kunming University of Science and Technology in 2019, and I completed my Master of Engineering degree there in 2022. My main research focus is on multi-robot path planning based on deep reinforcement learning. I am passionately exploring innovative methods to harness deep reinforcement learning for efficiently coordinating multiple robots, aiming to tackle complex real-world tasks and enhance system performance. As I embark on my doctoral journey in 2024, I look forward to contributing novel insights and methodologies to the field of intelligent robotics and automation.
